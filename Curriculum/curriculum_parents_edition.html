“Every child is a seed. We're the good soil.”
Good Soil was born from over a decade of hands-on aeromodelling workshops with Airmodelcrafts (AMC). We learned that what truly ignites young minds is not memorizing textbook diagrams — it's the thrill of building something with their own hands, troubleshooting when it doesn't work the first time, and seeing it take flight.
The 4-Phase Learning Pathway Progressive Skill Building
Phase 1 · Classes 01–06

Spark & Circuits

Understanding electric loops, battery polarity, switches, and breadboarding. Concludes with Milestone Challenge 1: Island Escape.

Phase 2 · Classes 07–12

Sensing & Mobile Bots

Light sensors (LDR), sound buzzers, mechanical gearboxes, and building a rolling mobile obstacle rover. Milestone 2: STEAM Carnival Derby.

Phase 3 · Classes 13–18

Logic, Code & Flight

Microcontroller fundamentals, block/C++ loops, sensor feedback, and introductory aeromodelling flight dynamics. Milestone 3: Sky Patrol.

Phase 4 · Classes 19–25

Kinematics, Drones & Expo

Bionic linkages, hydraulic robot arms, mini-drone avionics, solar energy, and the Grand Capstone Expo with parents.

Inside a 120-Minute Good Soil Class Structure & Discipline
1. Discovery & Starter (25 min)

Short visual story and tactile exploration of loose parts. Kids formulate a hypothesis before touching any assembly steps.

2. Guided Project Build (55 min)

Step-by-step physical construction with real tools. Safe wiring, mechanical alignment, and mentor-guided calibration.

3. Test, Flight & Passport (40 min)

Bench testing, timed trials, obstacle trials, and logging measurements in their personal student STEM Passport.

Our Educational Commitment to Families The Good Soil Promise
🎁 Real Take-Home Builds Every Child Keeps

Unlike programs where models are dismantled at the end of class, Good Soil students permanently take home 10 complete STEM builds — empowering them to demonstrate their creations to parents and continue tinkering at home.

🚫 100% Non-Paper Milestone Evaluations

We replace written tests with gamified build challenges (Island Escape, Rescue Rover Derby, Sky Patrol). Children prove their mastery by building, troubleshooting, and presenting live working prototypes.

🛡️ Safety-First & Small Cohorts (Max 8–10)

With an intimate 1:8 mentor-to-student ratio, low-voltage child-safe power supplies, and strict eye-protection guidelines, every child receives personal attention, encouragement, and safe tool training.

🎓 STEM Passport & Showcase Expo

Every student receives a physical Good Soil STEM Passport stamped upon completing each milestone badge. Class 25 is a celebratory Grand Expo where students pitch their Passion Projects to visiting families.

Frequently Asked Questions & Studio Policies Safety & Structure
Do kids need prior coding or robotics experience?

No prior background is required! Our curriculum begins with tactile first-principles (closed loops, polarity, levers) before introducing block-coding and microcontrollers. Every build is guided step-by-step by experienced mentors.

What are the batch schedules & format options?

We offer 3 flexible schedules:
• Weekend Classes: 2 hours every Saturday or Sunday (regular year-round progress).
• 2-Day Workshops: Intensive 3 hrs/day over long weekends & holidays.
• 5-Day Holiday Camps: 4 hrs/day immersion during summer, winter & spring breaks.

How are safety and tools handled in the workshop?

All electrical builds use safe, low-voltage DC batteries (3V to 9V) — no mains electricity is ever handled by students. For tools (screwdrivers, wire strippers, pliers), students receive dedicated safety briefings and work under direct 1:8 supervision with eye protection.

What happens if a child misses a weekend session?

We provide dedicated 45-minute catch-up clinics before the start of the next weekend's class, ensuring no student falls behind or misses out on assembling their take-home kit.
