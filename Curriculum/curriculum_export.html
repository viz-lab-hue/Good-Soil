Milestone Assessment 100-Point Scoring Rubric & Methodology Non-Paper Evaluation • Practical Competency
1. Engineering Build (30 Pts) Assembly precision, solderless breadboard discipline, smooth gear mesh, zero short-circuits.
2. First-Principles (25 Pts) Student verbally explains the underlying physics, current loops, gear ratio math, or flight forces.
3. Troubleshooting (20 Pts) Methodical fault tracing: multimeter testing, loose jumper identification, trim adjustment.
4. STEAM Extension (15 Pts) Creative mechanism enhancements, custom chassis tailoring, aerodynamic optimization.
5. Presentation (10 Pts) Clear articulation of technical decisions, teamwork, peer collaboration, and defense.
