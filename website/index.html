Where we come from

Grown from 10+ years of aeromodelling workshops.

Good Soil grew out of Airmodelcrafts (AMC) — a decade of teaching kids to build and fly across schools and communities. We saw what lit them up wasn't the theory. It was the moment their own plane left their hands.

So we took that spark and widened the field: robotics, DIY science, arts, tools — the whole garden.
