The Passion Project: build the thing only you would build.

Kids pick a problem they care about — a plant that waters itself, a drone that drops seeds, a toy for a little sibling — and design their own solution with everything they've learned.

01 Notice Find a problem at home, school or in nature.
02 Imagine Sketch wild ideas, then pick the best one.
03 Design Choose parts from the kit wall and plan the build.
04 Build & test Prototype, break it, fix it, make it better.
05 Pitch Present it at Showcase Day — to parents and industry guests.
