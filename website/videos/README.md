# Good Soil — Workshop Video & Reel Directory

Drop your edited phone videos and workshop recordings into this directory (`website/videos/`).

### Suggested Filenames & Slots:

1. **`hero-reel.mp4`** (Horizontal 16:9 or Square)
   - Used for: The main Hero Section video player on the homepage (`index.html`).
   - Length: ~30 to 60 seconds.
   - Recommended resolution: 1080p (1920x1080) or 720p (1280x720).

2. **`reel-1.mp4`** (Vertical 9:16)
   - Used for: **Glider Launch & Flight Trials** (Aeromodelling)
   - Length: ~10 to 20 seconds.
   - Aspect Ratio: 9:16 (Standard smartphone recording: 1080x1920).

3. **`reel-2.mp4`** (Vertical 9:16)
   - Used for: **Autonomous Rover Obstacle Run** (Robotics)
   - Length: ~15 to 25 seconds.
   - Aspect Ratio: 9:16.

4. **`reel-3.mp4`** (Vertical 9:16)
   - Used for: **Mini Drone Flight Practice** (Drones & Avionics)
   - Length: ~15 to 25 seconds.
   - Aspect Ratio: 9:16.

5. **`reel-4.mp4`** (Vertical 9:16)
   - Used for: **Circuit Crafter & Sensor Wiring** (Electronics & Breadboards)
   - Length: ~10 to 20 seconds.
   - Aspect Ratio: 9:16.

6. **`reel-5.mp4`** (Vertical 9:16)
   - Used for: **Showcase Pitch Day** (Passion Project demo to parents)
   - Length: ~20 to 35 seconds.
   - Aspect Ratio: 9:16.

---

### How It Works:
The website layout is pre-wired to look for these files. While you are editing them, the site automatically displays high-resolution photo posters with play buttons. Once you drop your `.mp4` or `.webm` files here with these names, they will play directly inside the custom modal player!
