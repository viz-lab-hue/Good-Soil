How the kits work

Every format mixes three kinds of kits, so kids learn to build alone and together.

Giveaway kits

Small builds they finish in class and carry home to fly, display, and tinker with.

Group lab kits

Bigger shared sets for team challenges, heavy robotic builds, and collaborative design.

Individual kits

A personal kit per child for weekend tracks and camps, taken home complete upon finish.
