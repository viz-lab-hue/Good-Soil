### Technical Placement Guidelines:
1. **Clamp Height:** Mount projector 1.4m – 1.6m above the table surface on the rear left corner.
2. **Angle of Incidence:** 35° to 45° downward angle to illuminate top wing surfaces without casting shadows from viewers in front.
3. **Ambient Light Shielding:** If outdoors, ensure booth canopy provides full overhead shade. High-contrast neon cyan (`#00FFFF`) and safety green (`#39FF14`) visuals punch cleanly through normal indoor hall lighting.
4. **Software Loop:** Run video / HTML5 canvas on full-screen borderless window. Set playback to infinite loop with hardware acceleration enabled.

## 3. Mentor Pitch & Lead Conversion Playbook

### Phase 1: The Kid Hook (0 – 10 Seconds)
*Never ask "Do you want to join a class?". Focus on the physical action.*
> **Glider Hook:** *"Hey! Think you can fly this balsa plane through that yellow hoop in two tries?"*  
> **Hydraulic Hook:** *"Grab that blue syringe! Push it down to open the robotic claw and see if you can pick up a candy."*  
> **Projection Hook:** *"Look at the wings on this aircraft — tilt the tail up and watch what happens to the airflow lines!"*

---

### Phase 2: The Parent Bridge (15 – 45 Seconds)
*Engage the parent while their child is immersed and completely focused on the build.*
> **Mentor:** *"Hi! Notice how your child hasn't looked at a phone or screen once since walking up?  
> We're **Good Soil** — Delhi NCR's dedicated hands-on maker academy for kids aged 7 to 14+. Everything here is 100% screen-free. In our weekend classes, kids work with real wood, micro-circuits, and aerodynamics — and take every build home."*

---

### Phase 3: The Founding Cohort & Heritage Pitch (45 – 90 Seconds)
> **Mentor:** *"We were born out of 10+ years of Delhi aeromodelling workshops (Airmodelcrafts). Rather than big coaching centers with 30 kids, our founding weekend cohorts are strictly capped at **6 to 8 students** with direct lead engineer mentorship."*

---

### Phase 4: The Low-Friction Call-to-Action (Closing)
*Never ask for an on-spot payment. Get them onto WhatsApp.*
> **Mentor:** *"Here is the glider your child just finished — it's theirs to keep!  
> Scan this QR code right here on your phone, or give me your WhatsApp number — I'll send you our complete **25-Class Master Syllabus Guide** and invite you to our upcoming weekend lab open house."*

## 4. Emergency On-Site Troubleshooting Quick-Guide

| Problem | Immediate Fix |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Projector image washed out** | Rotate stall table 90° away from direct spotlights; increase projector contrast to 85%; switch background to pure black (`#000000`). |
| **Hydraulic syringe has air bubble** | Submerge syringe tip in water cup, pump 3 times to bleed air, secure tubing with zip-tie. |
| **Glider dives into ground** | Gently bend trailing edge of elevator slightly upwards (positive decalage / up-trim). |
| **Crowd bottleneck at table** | Divert waiting children to the Flight Hoop Target zone; give them pre-folded paper gliders while waiting. |
